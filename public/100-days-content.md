## 7 Content Concepts

1. 🔥 **Contrarian Design:** Challenge an assumption. E.g., "Not every website needs dark mode."
2. 👀 **Design Detective:** Show UI, ask what is wrong, reveal the problem.
3. 🧪 **Design Experiment:** Document testing AI vs Human, Figma, etc.
4. ⚔️ **A vs B:** Genuine design trade-offs (Tabs vs disclosure).
5. 🧠 **Design Insight:** Principle → Real problem → Real example → Practical lesson. 
6. 🏗️ **Build in Public:** Show real work, redesigns, iterations.
7. 💬 **Learn From Experts:** Specific questions for senior designers to answer.

## Workflow
1. Select Concept 1-100.
2. Choose best of 7 formats.
3. Build Curiosity.
4. Build Tension.
5. Deliver Insight.
6. Provide Visual Proof.
7. Give Personal Perspective.
8. Ask meaningful discussion question.
